## Military Operations

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**Pentagon prepares plans for weeks of US ground operations in Iran.** *(Al Jazeera)*</summary>

The Pentagon has readied plans for limited ground operations in Iran's strategic regions that could last for weeks, according to Al Jazeera reporting. Uncertainty remains over whether President Trump will approve the Pentagon's proposed ground operation plans. This potential escalation would mark a significant shift from the air and missile strikes that have characterized the 30-day conflict to date.

Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/3/29/pentagon-readies-for-weeks-of-us-ground-operations-in-iran-report)

</details>

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**Iran strikes industrial zone in southern Israel, causing chemical plant fire.** *(Al Jazeera)*</summary>

An Iranian missile barrage hit an industrial zone near Beersheba in southern Israel, causing a fire at a chemical plant. This attack demonstrates Iran's continued ability to strike Israeli territory despite 30 days of US-Israeli military operations.

Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/3/29/iran-hits-industrial-zone-in-southern-israel)

</details>

Iran has reportedly struck US E-3 Sentry AWACS aircraft and air tankers, according to Al Jazeera. The report indicates Iran has targeted multiple US assets over the past month.

## Diplomacy

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**Pakistan hosts four-nation talks seeking de-escalation of Iran conflict.** *(Al Jazeera)*</summary>

Foreign ministers from Pakistan, Turkey, Egypt, and Saudi Arabia convened in Islamabad on day 30 of the conflict to encourage diplomatic engagement between the US and Iran. Pakistan is maintaining what Al Jazeera characterizes as a "delicate balancing act" in hosting these talks. The four-nation initiative aims to prepare the ground for de-escalation and encourage both sides toward diplomacy, though no concrete outcomes have been reported.

## Regional Actors

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**Houthis launch first attacks on Israel, opening new front in regional war.** *(Al Jazeera)*</summary>

The Houthi movement in Yemen has launched its first attacks on Israel, opening a new front in the month-long regional conflict. Al Jazeera reports that a potential Houthi blockade of the Bab al-Mandeb strait—one of the world's busiest maritime routes—would prove disastrous for the global economy. This expansion of the conflict zone raises concerns about further regional destabilization and economic disruption, compounding the ongoing Strait of Hormuz crisis.

## Economy

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**20,000 seafarers stranded in Strait of Hormuz as maritime crisis deepens.** *(Al Jazeera)*</summary>

International Maritime Organization chief Arsenio Dominguez warned that approximately 20,000 seafarers remain stranded in the Strait of Hormuz. Dominguez stated that "we can insure the ship, but we cannot insure a human life," highlighting the human toll of the maritime disruption. This crisis compounds the economic impact of the conflict, particularly with the potential Houthi blockade of Bab al-Mandeb threatening to close a second critical maritime chokepoint.

Sources: [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/video/newsfeed/2026/3/29/we-can-insure-the-ship-but-we-cannot-insure-a-human-life)

</details>

## Israel Policy

<details markdown="block">
<summary markdown="span">**Israeli police detain up to 18 anti-war protesters in Tel Aviv.** *(Al Jazeera)* [SINGLE SOURCE - UNCONFIRMED]</summary>

Police in Tel Aviv dispersed protesters opposing the US-Israeli war on Iran on Saturday, arresting between 12 and 18 people according to Al Jazeera reporting. The protest turned violent before police intervention. This represents domestic opposition within Israel to the ongoing military operations.
